Reference image is the existing approved plump white cat; preserve its recognizable round body, pointed ears, black eyes and tiny mouth, monochrome crisp pixel silhouette. Create a NEW action sheet, not a UI mockup. The curious cat sits on the LEFT and repeatedly reaches its RIGHT front paw far to the RIGHT as if gently testing and tapping a progress bar placed immediately to its right. DO NOT draw the progress bar, bowl, food, impact marks, captions, grid lines, numbers, or any other objects; the application draws the bar separately. Exactly FOUR columns and TWO rows, eight evenly spaced, equal square cells in reading order, ideally a wide 1536x768 image. Cat body and grounded feet remain anchored at exactly the same position and scale in all eight cells with generous transparent padding. Keep the entire paw fully inside each cell. Frame 1: curious seated cat with right paw resting. Frame 2: head tilts toward right and paw lifts. Frame 3: paw reaches halfway right. Frame 4: paw extends almost fully right. Frame 5: paw fully outstretched horizontally right, tapping an imaginary object at the right edge of its reach. Frame 6: paw recoils slightly but stays raised. Frame 7: paw draws back toward chest. Frame 8: paw returns to seated pose, matching frame 1 for a seamless loop. Paw silhouette and changing elbow positions must be obvious at tiny resolution. Cat body solid white, dark cutouts only for facial features, no gray shadows, no painterly shading. Actual fully transparent background with alpha, including between cells. No checkerboard drawn into pixels. Pixel-art style consistent with reference.
